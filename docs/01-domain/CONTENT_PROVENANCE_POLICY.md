# Content Provenance Policy

Status: Accepted

## Purpose

Educational trust is a release boundary. A polished interface cannot compensate for unknown, copied, or fabricated exam content.

## Source Priority

1. Official paper, answer key, and correction published by the responsible examination authority
2. Official annual registration or choice-selection booklet
3. Approved primary academic reference for explanatory material
4. Independently reviewed expert interpretation linked to primary evidence
5. Third-party archives for discovery only

Third-party possession does not make an artifact official.

## Source States

- `pending-acquisition`
- `acquired-unverified`
- `legacy-unverified`
- `partially-verified`
- `verified`
- `disputed`
- `unavailable`

URLs, local files, checksums, identity review, content coverage, and redistribution permission are separate facts. A URL alone is not acquisition. A checksum alone is not authority.

## Immutable History

- Sources and educational records use stable IDs and versioned revisions.
- Corrections append history and never overwrite the original key.
- Publication, withdrawal, and supersession are auditable.
- A published question always resolves to the exact source revisions that supported it.

## Publication Gates

### Raw Question

Requires verified paper identity, reviewed transcription or image representation, source location, option integrity, and accessibility handling for images.

### Official Answer

Requires a verified official key and all applicable verified corrections. Konkur explanations cannot establish correctness.

### Classification And Frequency

Requires reviewed question-to-topic mappings and a documented calculation over declared coverage. Missing coverage remains unknown.

### Learning Asset

Requires source links, educational review, language review where applicable, and product QA. Concise assets are not exempt from correctness review.

## Generated Material

AI- or template-generated content records its inputs and generation version. It remains a draft until a human editor approves it. Generated text may explain an official answer but cannot create one.

## Independently Authored Questions

- Commercial learner questions are original Konkur works supported by approved academic references and factual official exam metadata.
- Authorship, reference revisions, subject review, language review, and originality review are recorded.
- Official or third-party questions may not be used as hidden templates for transcription, translation, paraphrase, or near-copying.
- Konkur-authored answers are never labeled `official-answer`, and authored questions are never presented as past-paper questions.
- Historical frequency and occurrence claims remain unknown unless a separate rights-cleared evidence contract supports them.

## Copyright And Redistribution

Before storing or publicly serving an artifact, record its owner, source URL, retrieval date, known license or legal basis, allowed uses, and restrictions. Where redistribution is uncertain, store metadata and links without publishing the file until legal review decides.

## User Reports

Every published question and explanation must support an issue report tied to a stable revision. Critical correctness disputes can withdraw affected content without deleting audit history.

## Release Metrics

Each vertical release contract must declare expected source coverage, verified question coverage, reviewed explanation coverage, open disputes, and known missing years. Percentages never conceal an unknown denominator.
