# ADR-0027: AI As Optional Accelerator

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-08-27

## Decision

AI may assist content operations and grounded learner capabilities but is neither an educational authority nor a core availability dependency. Provider-neutral interfaces, local models, deterministic fallbacks, review gates, budgets, and kill switches are required.

## Consequences

Core account, learning, exam, commerce, and Admin workflows remain functional during provider failure. Generated content cannot publish automatically.
