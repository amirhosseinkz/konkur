# General Availability Scope

Status: Accepted baseline

## Launch Definition

GA is the first public commercial release. It is not an MVP, beta, preview, or experiment exposed as a finished product.

GA combines a production-complete platform with one content-complete vertical: Computer Engineering MSc. Other exam families may be visible only when their public information is accurate and their unavailable status is explicit.

## Included

### Public Website

- Persian-first responsive marketing and exam pages
- Technical SEO, sitemap, canonical URLs, structured data, and share metadata
- Pricing, policies, support entry points, authentication, and checkout
- Reviewed public samples and noindex controls for private content

### Learner Website

- Account creation, authentication, recovery, export, and deletion
- Exam selection, exam date, available time, target, and onboarding
- Diagnostic flow and transparent recommendation inputs
- Personal study plan with user override
- Lessons, question practice, flashcards, bookmarks, notes, and search
- Practice, exam, review, and final-review modes
- Negative marking, corrections, deleted questions, multiple accepted answers, timing, autosave, and recovery
- Mistake history, review queue, progress, and responsive mobile/desktop behavior
- Billing and entitlement management

### Admin Website

- Exam catalog and annual scope management
- Official source inventory, checksums, provenance, and revision history
- Question, answer, correction, topic, and learning-asset workflows
- Draft, review, approval, publication, withdrawal, and rollback
- Role-based access, audit logs, support tooling, and commerce visibility

### Platform Operations

- Production environments and automated deployment
- Database migrations, backup, restore, monitoring, alerting, and incident response
- Payment processing, refunds, entitlement reconciliation, and failure recovery
- Product analytics and consent-aware event collection
- Security, accessibility, performance, and load evidence

### Content Vertical

Computer Engineering MSc must have the independently authored, academically source-backed, and reviewed learning coverage declared by the content release contract created during Milestone 1. Official artifacts provide factual exam metadata and canonical links only; their questions, keys, corrections, and page images are not part of the commercial corpus. Legacy-unverified prototype content is not automatically eligible.

## Excluded From GA

- Native iOS, Android, or desktop learner applications
- Doctoral entrance exams
- Live tutoring marketplace
- Social feed, chat community, or user-generated answer keys
- Guaranteed rank, admission probability, or unsupported score prediction
- AI-only functionality without deterministic fallback
- Automatic publication of generated educational content
- Full commercial coverage of every Iranian entrance exam before its content passes the same gates

## Internal Delivery Rule

Milestones 0 through 7 are internal. No milestone may be marketed as a production release. Controlled research sessions may use non-production environments with explicit participant consent and no production claims.

## Expansion Rule

After GA, a new exam vertical is commercially enabled only when its source, content, exam behavior, support, pricing entitlement, and QA gates pass independently. New verticals reuse the platform; they do not fork it.
