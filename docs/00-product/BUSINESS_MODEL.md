# Business Model

Status: Principles accepted; price points pending research

## Revenue Thesis

Learners pay for trusted prioritization, complete exam workflows, high-quality source-backed content, adaptive review, and saved preparation time. They do not pay merely for access to PDFs or a large question count.

## Customer

The initial customer and user are usually the same candidate. Family payment, institutional sales, and advisor tooling are future channels and must not distort the initial learner experience.

## Entitlements

Access is modeled through independent entitlements:

- exam family;
- annual exam scope;
- exam group or set;
- capability set;
- validity period;
- promotional or support override.

Plan names never appear in domain authorization logic.

## Packaging Hypotheses

The launch research must evaluate:

- Free Preview with meaningful but bounded value;
- a 90-day Exam Pass;
- a 180-day Exam Pass;
- monthly access where recurring value is defensible;
- annual or All Access only when multiple complete verticals create real year-round value.

Lifetime access is not a default because exam content and operations have recurring costs.

## Pricing Rules

- Do not set prices from intuition alone.
- Research willingness to pay and substitute products.
- Keep taxes, gateway fees, refunds, support, content operations, infrastructure, and AI costs visible.
- Avoid dark patterns, hidden renewal, artificial countdowns, and misleading discounts.
- Show entitlement duration and renewal behavior before payment.
- Define a clear refund and payment-failure policy.

## Unit Economics

Before GA, Finance must approve measured or bounded values for acquisition cost, conversion, gateway loss, refund rate, support cost, infrastructure cost per active learner, content cost per vertical, gross margin, and expected entitlement duration.

No unbounded per-request AI cost is allowed. AI budgets must be capped per capability and have a non-AI fallback.

## Commercial Integrity

- Payment does not alter source or educational verification status.
- A learner who loses entitlement retains legally required account, invoice, and progress access.
- Support overrides are audited.
- Promotions are modeled explicitly and expire deterministically.
- Refunds reconcile payment, entitlement, and invoice state.
