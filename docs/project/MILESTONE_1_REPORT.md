## Material Findings

- Official exam identity is established for 1397-1404.
- The 1403 paper has a live official notice and artifact chain.
- No target year has a complete verified official paper, authoritative key, and correction history.
- No intended full-artifact or question-level commercial use has recorded redistribution or transformation permission.
- The repository contains no official source artifact; Prototype Zero URLs and extracted answers remain third-party `legacy-unverified` evidence.
- Sanjesh pages that initially returned 403 became accessible on retry. Access is therefore intermittent, not unavailable.
- Direct competitors sell instruction and mock-exam packages, while broad libraries and free papers/videos are meaningful substitutes. Public prices do not establish demand.
- Customer need, willingness to pay, content throughput, and unit cost have not yet been measured and remain explicit later-stage evidence gates.

## Accepted Decisions

ADR-0029 permits metadata and canonical links by default and blocks artifact retention, mirroring, OCR, transcription, translation, and question-level transformation until written source-owner permission or qualified Iranian legal approval covers the intended use.

ADR-0030 removes that external dependency from GA. Konkur's commercial corpus uses independently authored questions and academic references and never reproduces or labels official questions or answers.
