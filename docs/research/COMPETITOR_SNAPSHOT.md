## Product Implications To Test

- Candidates may compare Konkur with both expensive comprehensive instruction and inexpensive broad libraries.
- Trusted prioritization, source visibility, complete exam behavior, mistake review, and saved planning time may be differentiators, but this remains a hypothesis until candidate research.
- Promotional prices are widespread in the snapshot; Konkur must not copy artificial countdowns or misleading discount anchors.
- Free paper and video substitutes make raw access a weak standalone paid value proposition.

This snapshot is selective, not proof that no other competitor exists.
