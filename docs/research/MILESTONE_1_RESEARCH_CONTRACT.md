# Milestone 1 Research Contract

Status: Complete

Date opened: 2026-08-27

Date closed: 2026-08-28

## Objective

Determine whether a trustworthy, commercially operated Computer Engineering MSc vertical is feasible and define the evidence contract required before production architecture begins.

## Questions

### Sources And Rights

- Which official papers, keys, corrections, and annual catalog documents can be identified for the target years?
- Which artifacts can be linked, acquired internally, transcribed, transformed, or redistributed?
- What legal or permission questions remain unresolved?
- What safe default applies before formal legal clearance?

### Content Feasibility

- What exact source and editorial coverage is required for GA?
- Which Prototype Zero assets are reusable only as drafts, and which are not reusable?
- What roles, review stages, throughput, and correction behavior are required?

### Market And Customer

- What job is the candidate hiring the product to do?
- What substitutes and direct competitors exist?
- Which claims can be tested before GA through private research?
- What evidence is required to select packages and price points?

### Economics

- What are the primary variable and fixed costs?
- Which costs scale per learner, question, exam year, or vertical?
- How will pricing protect gross margin without relying on unbounded AI usage?

## Evidence Classes

| Class | Meaning | Decision use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Official publisher, regulator, statute, or provider documentation | May support a decision, subject to interpretation limits |
| B | Direct observation of a competitor's current public product or pricing | May support market comparison, not demand claims |
| C | Structured interview, usability session, or pricing research with recorded method | May support customer hypotheses within sample limits |
| D | Prototype analytics or founder experience | Generates hypotheses; does not establish market-wide behavior |
| E | Unverified third-party summary or search result | Discovery only |

Every external evidence record includes title, publisher, URL, access date, evidence class, extracted fact, and limitations.

## Research Integrity

- Do not invent market size, conversion, willingness to pay, legal rights, or source coverage.
- Competitor absence from a search is not proof that no competitor exists.
- Public pricing is a snapshot, not proof of transaction volume.
- Founder success is valuable product evidence but not a representative outcome claim.
- Legal findings are operational risk analysis, not legal advice.
- Automated fetching does not grant redistribution rights.

## Required Outputs

- Official-source inventory and gap report
- Operational source-rights decision and unresolved legal questions
- Computer Engineering MSc GA content release contract
- Customer, competitor, and pricing research plan
- Current competitor snapshot with dated evidence
- Content operations model and cost model template
- Updated pending decisions and risk register
- Milestone report with limitations and next-stage constraints

## Exit Rule

Milestone 1 may close when all roadmap evidence exists, the validator passes, remaining unknowns have explicit owners and deadlines, and no unresolved issue prevents architecture work. Closure does not authorize public redistribution or production launch.

## Outcome

ADR-0030 removes official-artifact acquisition and redistribution from the GA dependency path. The accepted release contract uses independently authored questions and academic references. Historical source gaps remain documented but do not prevent architecture. Candidate pricing fieldwork remains owned by Product for Milestone 6, and measured content throughput remains owned by Content/Operations as a Milestone 4 scale gate.
